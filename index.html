about

The Gospel Pursuit

A study Bible for people meeting scripture for the first time — and for those who want to go deeper. Read any book chapter by chapter, understand what a passage means, follow a plan, and grow a daily habit. This app is in beta: some study notes are still being refined, and your feedback shapes what comes next.

A note on our study notes

We want you to trust what you read here, so we tell you where it comes from:

  • Original-language tools — the Hebrew and Greek behind a verse, and the word-by-word meanings — are drawn from real, established texts and lexicons. You can lean on them.
  • Context & meaning, chapter overviews, and book overviews are AI-assisted study aids. They're a helpful guide, but they can be imperfect — always weigh them against the text of scripture itself. Notes carrying a green “Reviewed” mark have been checked by a person against a study Bible.
  • Found something that reads wrong? Please use the Report button in the app — corrections to biblical accuracy and theological context are exactly what we most want to hear.

Your privacy

The short version: your study stays yours, and we don't sell your data.

  • On your device. Your reading progress, notes, highlights, saved verses, plans and settings are stored locally in your browser. They stay on your device unless you choose to sign in.
  • Optional account. If you sign in, we use a third-party sign-in provider (Clerk) to identify you and sync that same study data across your devices. Signing in is never required to use the app.
  • Study content. When you open a passage or a study note, the app asks our server for it; the server uses AI to prepare overviews and context. We don't attach these requests to advertising profiles.
  • Reminders. Daily reading reminders (web push) are opt-in and can be turned off at any time in your browser or device settings.
  • No ad tracking. We don't run third-party advertising trackers.

Questions about your data, or want it removed? Reach us through the in-app Report button.
